### About me

I'm a Software Engineering student at Jiangxi Normal University, interested in **gameplay programming**, **game AI**, and the space where they meet. I enjoy turning ideas into playable prototypes and exploring how intelligent agents can create richer interactions.

- 🎮 Building games with **Unreal Engine**, C++ and Blueprints, and exploring 2D development with Unity.
- 🧠 Exploring reinforcement learning, behavior trees, and AI-driven NPC interactions.
- 🔬 Interested in designing game AI experiments and bringing research ideas into playable worlds.
